LexiCard: Integrated Flashcards & Notes

A UX case study on designing a smart learning app that fuses spaced repetition flashcards with deeply integrated notes to boost long-term retention for students and lifelong learners.

Project Overview

My Role

Product Designer, UX Researcher

Duration

12 weeks

Tools

Figma, Miro, Notion

Project Type

Personal Portfolio Project

Problem Statement

Modern learners are forced to use separate apps for flashcards (like Anki or Quizlet) and note-taking (like Notion or GoodNotes). This digital fragmentation creates friction, preventing them from building a connected knowledge base where detailed notes directly support memory recall, leading to inefficient study and forgotten context.

User Personas

Based on initial research, I developed two primary personas to guide the design process: a university student facing heavy academic loads and a professional dedicated to continuous learning.

Chloe, The University Student

Demographics

  • Age: 21
  • Occupation: Medical Student
  • Location: Boston, MA

Goals

  • Efficiently memorize vast amounts of information for exams.
  • Understand the "why" behind facts, not just rote memorization.
  • Keep all study materials for a single subject in one place.

Frustrations

  • "I make flashcards in one app, but all my detailed diagrams and lecture notes are in another. I constantly switch between them."
  • "When I review a flashcard and forget the context, I have to manually search through my notes, which breaks my study flow."
  • "Anki is powerful but ugly and complicated to set up."

Mark, The Lifelong Learner

Demographics

  • Age: 35
  • Occupation: Software Engineer
  • Location: Remote

Goals

  • Learn new programming languages and frameworks for career growth.
  • Retain knowledge for the long term, not just for a specific project.
  • Easily capture and revisit interesting concepts from articles and books.

Frustrations

  • "I have a graveyard of book notes in Notion that I never look at again."
  • "Creating flashcards feels like a chore. I wish I could highlight something in my notes and have it automatically become a reviewable item."
  • "I want to see how small concepts connect to a larger topic, but my tools don't support this easily."

Competitor Analysis

The market is divided between powerful but separate tools. No single app seamlessly integrates active recall (flashcards) with knowledge management (notes). This gap is LexiCard's primary opportunity.

Competitor Category Strengths Weaknesses
Anki Flashcards Gold-standard spaced repetition algorithm, highly customizable. Steep learning curve, dated UI, poor note-taking features.
Quizlet Flashcards User-friendly, social features, multiple study modes. Weaker algorithm, subscription-heavy, very basic notes.
Notion Notes/PKM Extremely flexible, powerful databases, great for organization. No native spaced repetition system (requires workarounds).
Obsidian Notes/PKM Local-first, fantastic linking/graph view, extensible with plugins. Flashcard plugins exist but are not core to the experience.
RemNote Hybrid One of the few apps that tries to integrate both. Complex UI can be overwhelming, less polished than competitors.
GoodNotes Notes Excellent handwriting/PDF annotation experience. No flashcard or spaced repetition functionality.

MVP & Key Features

To address the core problem, the Minimum Viable Product (MVP) focused on four critical features that create a unified learning loop.

Integrated Note & Card Editor

Create rich notes with text, images, and lists. Seamlessly highlight any content to instantly convert it into a flashcard without leaving the note.

Smart Review System

A dedicated review queue, powered by a spaced repetition algorithm, pulls cards from all your notes into a single, daily study session.

Bi-Directional Context Linking

Every flashcard is automatically linked back to its source note. When reviewing, tap a button to instantly see the card in its original context, eliminating confusion.

Organized Knowledge Hub

Group your notes and card sets into subjects or "Libraries." A clean dashboard provides a high-level view of your learning progress and upcoming reviews.

Information Architecture

The app's structure was designed to be simple and scalable, centered around a main navigation bar that provides quick access to the four primary user contexts.

Brand Strategy & Visual Design

The brand identity aims to be clean, trustworthy, and motivating, guided by the "Tech Minimal + Comfortable Learning" philosophy. I explored three distinct visual directions through stylescapes to find the perfect balance.

Vision & Mission

Vision: To create the most efficient and integrated platform for building lasting knowledge. Mission: To empower learners by seamlessly connecting deep understanding with long-term memory.

Brand Personality

Supportive: A helpful guide for learning. Intelligent: Smart and efficient. Calm: A focused, low-stress environment. Minimal: Uncluttered and straight to the point.

Design Principles

Clarity over clutter. Flow over friction. Integration over separation. Consistency over novelty.

Stylescape Explorations

Direction 1: "Focus" - Tech Minimal

This direction uses a monochromatic palette with a single, vibrant accent color (Teal). It prioritizes clarity, legibility, and a calm, scientific feel, inspired by the provided color guidelines.

Direction 2: "Growth" - Organic & Approachable

This direction uses a warmer, more organic palette (soft greens, beige). It aims to feel more friendly and encouraging, using rounded corners and softer typography to reduce intimidation.

Direction 3: "Momentum" - Dark Mode & Energetic

This direction is built for dark mode first, using high-contrast, energetic accents (bright blues, purples) to create a modern, dynamic feel. It's designed for users who are motivated by progress and data visualization.

Final UI & High-Fidelity Prototype

After feedback, I chose the "Focus" (Tech Minimal) direction for its superior clarity and alignment with the brand's core principles. I applied this system to the wireframes to create the final high-fidelity designs and an interactive prototype.

Learnings & Next Steps

Key Learning

The biggest challenge was balancing feature richness with simplicity. Early feedback showed users were overwhelmed by too many options. This reinforced the "Clarity over Clutter" principle, leading to a more streamlined UI.

Future Feature: Collaboration

Enable users to share note libraries and flashcard decks with classmates or colleagues, turning solitary study into a collaborative effort.

Future Feature: AI Assistant

Integrate AI to automatically suggest flashcards from long-form notes and generate summaries, further reducing the friction of creating study materials.
