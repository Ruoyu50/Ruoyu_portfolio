The Challenge

Problem Statement

How might we allow college students and language learners to more easily memorize abstract vocabulary without the boredom of rote repetition?

Traditional flashcards lack context, leading to high dropout rates and "isolated knowledge" that is quickly forgotten.

Value Proposition

SceCards is the AI Narrative Platform
that allows Students
to Turn Vocab Lists into Stories.

Business Plan Summary

Freemium model offering basic AI generation (5 stories/day). Premium subscription unlocks unlimited stories, "Style Transfer" (e.g., Horror, Sci-Fi themes), and audio narration features.
