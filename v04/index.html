MentorBridge

View Prototype Read Story

The Challenge

Problem Statement

How might we allow college students to more easily find mentors that will help them with their careers?

Students currently rely on awkward networking events or cold-emailing alumni, resulting in low response rates and high anxiety.

Value Proposition

MentorBridge is the Career Acceleration Platform
that allows College Students
to Gain Industry Access & Guidance.

Business Plan Summary

Freemium model for students (basic matching) with Premium Subscription for universities to offer "white-label" mentorship programs to their alumni networks.

Discovery & Research

Competitor Analysis

Analyzed 10+ platforms including LinkedIn, ADPList, and Handshake.

Type Example Gap
Direct ADPList Too focused on Design/Tech only.
Secondary LinkedIn Intimidating; not mentorship-first.

Persona: The Anxious Achiever

Pain: Doesn't know "unwritten rules" of corporate.

Brand Strategy & Identity

Step 1: Brand Foundation

Vision: A world where every student has a champion in their corner.

Mission: To democratize access to career wisdom.

Personality: Empathetic, Knowledgeable, Ambitious, Transparent.

Design Principles: Clarity over clutter, Warmth in technology.

Validation & Testing

Methodology

  • Interview Discussion Guide created.
  • Feature Ranking Board exercise.
  • Moderated User Testing (Recorded).

Key Insights

  • Users were confused by the "Blind Match" feature.
  • Video intros increased trust significantly.
  • Booking flow needed fewer steps.
