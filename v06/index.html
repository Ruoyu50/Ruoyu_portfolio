Senior UX Portfolio Project

Scenes

An AI-driven narrative platform designed to help college students memorize abstract concepts through storytelling.

[Placeholder: High-Fidelity Hero Device Mockup]

Problem Definition

The Challenge

College students struggle to retain abstract vocabulary because traditional flashcards utilize rote memorization without context. This leads to disengagement and a rapid "forgetting curve."

How might we allow students to more easily memorize complex terms by embedding them into generated narratives?

Value Proposition

The Solution Formula

Scenes is the narrative learning engine that allows college students to transform isolated terms into memorable stories.

Market Research

Competitor Landscape

We analyzed the market to identify gaps in narrative-based learning tools.

Product Category Analysis
Anki Direct Powerful algorithms (spaced repetition) but extremely poor UI and zero narrative context.
Quizlet Secondary Gamified and social, but relies heavily on flipping cards without deeper connection.
Duolingo Indirect Great engagement loop, but rigid curriculum doesn't allow custom study decks.

Key Learnings

  • Insight 1: Users found the "Deck" metaphor confusing initially; renamed to "Story Packs."
  • Insight 2: Participants wanted manual control to edit the AI-generated story if it didn't make sense.
  • Insight 3: The loading animation was too static; users didn't know if the AI was working.
