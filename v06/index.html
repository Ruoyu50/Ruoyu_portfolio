UX Portfolio Project

Scenes App

A language experience platform that helps college students naturally encounter and internalize vocabulary in everyday life contexts.

Problem Statement

Challenge

College students often get fatigued in traditional vocabulary learning due to rote memorization lacking context and engagement.

How might we help college students encounter words more naturally, in context, and with minimal willpower?

Value Proposition

Solution

Scenes is a language experience platform that allows college students to naturally encounter and internalize words through everyday life moments.
